Draft legend
------------
(a) Representative theta-band (4-12 Hz) current source density (CSD) from a control (CTL), a PTEN
knockout (PTEN) and a PTEN/Raptor double knockout (DKO) mouse, 6 s each. Every even contact of the
64-contact linear probe (H3; even contacts 40 um apart) is shown, top = dorsal; black traces are the 4-12 Hz LFP on the
same contacts (same scale in all three). Sinks blue, sources red; one color scale for all three panels.
(b) Representative control dentate spike (300 ms window centered on the spike): LFP (5-100 Hz, 60 Hz
notch), CSD from the same filtered signals, and wavelet spectrograms (1-800 Hz, broadband with 60 Hz
notch) from four contacts (arrowheads). (c) Representative interictal epileptiform discharge (IED) from a
PTEN mouse (300 ms window): unfiltered LFP (60 Hz notch only), CSD of the same signals, and spectrograms
as in (b). CSD = -d2V/dz2 over the even contacts (conductivity omitted), end contacts duplicated
(Vaknin et al., 1988); bad contacts replaced by the mean of their even neighbors (CTL a: Ch 34 and 38;
c: Ch 8). Spectrograms: complex Morlet wavelets, 3 cycles at 1 Hz rising to 10 cycles at 800 Hz, power in
dB relative to each frequency's median over the surrounding 8 s. Data were downsampled from 30 kHz to
2.5 kHz with a zero-phase anti-alias filter; all filters are zero-phase (Butterworth, order 3; notch Q 30).
Scale bars: 50 ms; 1 mV (b), 2 mV (c). Even channel numbers and estimated cell layers mark depth
(SO, stratum oriens; SP, stratum pyramidale; SR, stratum radiatum; SLM, stratum lacunosum-moleculare;
ML, molecular layer; GCL, granule cell layer; HIL, hilus). Spectrogram frequency axes are logarithmic.
