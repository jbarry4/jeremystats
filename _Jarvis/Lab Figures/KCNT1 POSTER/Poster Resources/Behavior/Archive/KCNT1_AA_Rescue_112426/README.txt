     shocks P 0.033, same-cohort test P 0.011-0.031
  5  Young and old, per turn: young Hom improve like young WT (P 0.68-0.82), old Hom less than old WT (learning
     slopes P 0.015-0.044); Hom x age P 0.04-0.11 depending on measure and test
  6  Age within adults on one floor (19-46 weeks): no effect; extrapolating raw counts with three slopes: old Hom
     no worse than young Hom on day-2 shocks (P 0.16-0.36), trail them on improvement (P 0.036-0.066)
  7  Scoreboard: every age claim by every route; what can and cannot be said; what a settling experiment needs

Bottom line: the aging effect this data set supports is 'old Hom fail to improve' (same floor, and per turn),
not 'old Hom perform worse' (day 2 old Hom vs old WT P 0.4). Young vs old also differ in year and injection.

Red background on a panel or cell = the groups compared ran at different floor speeds.
