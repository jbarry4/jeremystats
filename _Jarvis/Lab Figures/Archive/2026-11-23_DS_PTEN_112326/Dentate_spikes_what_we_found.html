Dentate spikes in sparse PTEN knockout mice: what we found

7 controls and 9 PTEN mice (4 IED−, 5 IED+), one recording a mouse. Two event sets, Halo and Strict. A test battery of 121,000+ tests and about 4,500 held-out machine-learning fits.

m6 excluded. m51 too few events for statistics. m21 in the ML with no DS type.

Notes: Everything DS-related since the first deep round on 11-16: the classifier and DS3 work, battery runs 1 to 5, ML runs r005 to r017. Exploratory pilot; P values are per test.

The short version

Four main findings

1

DS1 and DS2 are real and classical

Models trained on other mice tell them apart at 0.77 to 0.80, from where the sink sits: DS1 farther out in the molecular layer.

2

PTEN spikes leave the control shape

A border drawn around control events keeps fewer PTEN events as knockout rises (ρ −0.75, P 0.001).

3

IED+ spikes are uniform and learnable

Models tell IED+ from IED− mice at 0.95 in both sets. IED+ spikes vary less in size and lean on the lower blade.

4

The sink profile tracks knockout

Within PTEN, the CSD just inside the main sink follows knockout at ρ 0.98 (P 0.00005).

What the models use

DS1 has more sink above the upper main sink (12 of 15 mice) and less just below it (13 of 15). Its share of sink at the outer contacts is higher in 13 of 15.

Without shape: DS1 comes when theta is lower (12 of 15 mice), with less gamma at the peak.

Notes: Depth axis: 0 = the upper blade's main sink, 1 = the lower blade's. Negative is above the upper blade, toward the outer molecular layer. Earlier hand work agreed: where the split is clean (high separation index: m26, m61, m33, m1, m28, m7), DS1 is 7 to 16% of events, its sink 0.3 to 0.5 times DS2's and 98 to 329 µm farther out, in both blades. That is the classical DS1 (lateral perforant path, outer layer). Elsewhere the events form one continuous cloud.

Where DS1 is a small far-out group, its events are under the noise floor (too small to tell from baseline), so DS3 only ever comes from DS2 here. The DS3 share does not differ by group (PTEN vs control P 0.95), but DS3 events lean on the lower blade in every PTEN mouse (P 0.016, both sets).

Notes: The noise floor is the 99th percentile of the same measure taken in quiet stretches at least 30 ms from any DS. Events under it are "unscorable", counted apart, never DS3. IED− mice have many unscorable events (IED+ vs IED− P 0.029), which is why they show almost no DS3.

The border check

PTEN dentate spikes more often fall outside the control-shaped cloud

ρ −0.75

Share of events the Strict border keeps, against knockout. Every high-knockout mouse is below every control (P 0.001).

The events the border drops are the lower-blade-heavy ones in IED+ mice (P 0.010), and they carry the weaker source with knockout too.

The P-spike border removes events almost only in IED+ mice: 4 of 5 lose 0.8–3.4%, against 1 of 7 controls (P 0.030).

The Strict border (10% grace) was drawn around control events, so PTEN events differ in shape from control events.

Notes: Results hold on both Halo and Strict, even though Strict removes some of exactly the events that carry the blade-balance and source findings.

The sink profile tracks knockout almost perfectly

The CSD just inside each blade’s main sink, relative to that sink

ρ 0.98

Strict set P 0.00005; Halo set ρ 0.95, P 0.0004. Nine PTEN mice. The strongest knockout relation of the 1,145 measures tested against knockout within PTEN.

Where it is measured

95% of the way from the cell-layer source to the main sink, both blades averaged, at the DS peak.

What it means

The shape of the sink’s inner edge changes steadily with the share of knockout granule cells.

Blade balance against knockout over all mice (P 0.008). Averaged curves agree: IED+ vs IED− cluster P 0.007 at the upper sink.

0.83

Best held-out models for PTEN vs control and for low vs high knockout put blade balance first (r006).

A change in one blade points at where the knockout cells are, or at one input being hit harder. Worth checking with histology: probe position in each blade, and knockout cells per blade.

Notes: Not every way of saying it holds: the absolute lower sink was not larger, only its share. In the controls the two blades are about equal; in all five IED+ mice the lower blade is the larger.

IED+ against IED−

IED+ dentate spikes are more uniform, and models tell IED+ from IED− mice

The battery

Sink size varies less from event to event: every IED+ mouse below every control (P 0.0025; Strict 0.030) and below IED− (P 0.016).

Within PTEN, events split less cleanly into two kinds as knockout rises (ρ −0.83, P 0.015). IED+ mice fire at a steadier pace (P 0.032).
