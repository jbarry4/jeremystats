# ds-pten-deep-round-1116

_2026-11-16 deep round on the Halo set: 240 tests give nothing after correction; DS1/DS2 resolve in 6 of 17 recordings (DS1 ~8%, outer by ~160 um, 0.4x sink); one lead (granule-layer source weakens with knockout). Figures on the Desktop._

The user called the 11-13 DS3 figure and result "mid" and asked for a deeper, wider look with publish-ready figures ("POLISH!"). Done 2026-11-16 on room v6 (Halo), checked on v8 (No P-spikes). One recording a mouse; m51 (5 events) left out of stats.

**What held up**
- The best split per animal (highest SI among the 15, 5%+ a side) resolves two families in 6 of 17: m26, m61 (CTL), m33, m1, m28, m7 (PTEN). The other 11 are one cloud whatever the split.
- In those six, DS1 is 7–16% of events (median 8%), its sink centre sits 105–275 µm farther from the granule-layer source than DS2's **in both blades** (the lower-blade shift is not forced by the naming rule, which only says "shallower main sink first"), and its deepest sink is ~0.4× DS2's. Against the other 11: 163 vs 59 µm, P 0.0006. Fits classical DS1 (outer, smaller) and Farrell 2024's ~11% DS1.
- DS3 by the Tarcsay score is a spacing artefact here: ~80% when the two contacts are 50–100 µm apart, ~0% at 200+ µm. In the six resolved recordings it is 0–18% (median 1%), and most DS1 events there are under the noise floor (unscorable).

**PTEN against control**
- 240 tests over 83 measures: best raw P 0.003; shuffling mouse labels does as well 41% of the time. 15 chosen measures: smallest BH q 0.36. Group cannot be decoded leave-one-mouse-out (AUC 0.38, P 0.60); knockout is ordered weakly (rho 0.55, P 0.03).
- The one lead: the net source between the blades (granule layer) weakens with % knockout relative to the sinks — all mice ρ −0.57 P 0.016, PTEN only ρ −0.78 P 0.013; six ways of measuring it agree in sign; control vs PTEN 15%+ d 1.29 (P 0.029 uncorrected). A planned test needs ~12 mice a group. **A lead, never a finding.**

**Round 4 (same day):** `quality_check_round4` (round 3 left in place) adds panel h: a line and a parabola on PC1/PC2 chosen to MAXIMISE the separation index (5% minimum a type; `deep_opt.py`), beside the conventional panel g, with events that change type ringed and a held-out index (fit on half, score the other half).
