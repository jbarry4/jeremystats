Asked for: "the Theta view is unfortunately the theta voltage raster view... we want it to be a theta CSD view with an option to adjust the smoothing", modelled on Xplorefinder's Csdview (CSDPP2's second difference, interp2, symmetric colour range). What a voltage raster and a CSD differ by, measurably: the second spatial derivative loses the outermost channel at each end, so a CSD of n channels has n-2 rows where a voltage raster has n. That is the check that cannot pass by accident. The rest is the smoothing: it must be reachable from the pane's own Panel menu, it must change the picture, and turning it to 0 must give the raw derivative back.
