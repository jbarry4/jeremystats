The three plots Toothy draws beside its channel picker — DS count, DS amplitude, DS height above surround — on an Incisor scan. Checked as pictures, not as function calls: that each canvas has ink on it, that the tallest bar in the count plot stands over the channel that actually has the most events, and that clicking a plot puts the hilus on the channel under the pointer. Then the other way of choosing one: that "Most spikes" names the argmax over the counts, which is a different channel from the scan's own pick whenever a channel carries many small events.

Three channels, not sixty-four. This is about the wiring and the picture; the arithmetic is checked against Toothy's own code in `tools/check_against_toothy_code.py`.
